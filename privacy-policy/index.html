This Privacy Policy explains how NeoNexus Innovations handles information when you visit the AXIOM website, use the AXIOM platform or contact us. We aim to handle information responsibly and in accordance with applicable laws.

1. Who we are

AXIOM is a business platform developed and operated by NeoNexus Innovations ("NeoNexus", "we", "us" or "our"). AXIOM supports business marketing, lead generation, lead management and sales-related workflows. This Policy applies to information handled through our website and the AXIOM services.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with AXIOM, we may collect information that you provide, such as your name, business contact details, organisation details, account information, enquiries and materials submitted through the Platform.

When AXIOM is used for business workflows, information may also include lead and customer contact details, enquiry information, follow-up records, campaign information, uploaded creative materials, messages and information received through connected services. Basic technical information relating to website or platform use may also be collected to support operation and security.

AXIOM currently does not record sales-call audio, store call recordings or generate call transcripts.

3. How we use information

We may use information to provide and maintain AXIOM, manage accounts, support marketing and sales workflows, process customer requests, enable connected features, provide AI-assisted functionality, communicate with users, maintain security, improve our services and meet legal obligations.

4. Cookies

Our website or Platform may use cookies or similar technologies to support essential functions such as navigation, security, session management and user preferences. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website or platform functions.

5. Sharing information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information where reasonably necessary to provide AXIOM or comply with law. This may include sharing with service providers that support hosting, infrastructure, storage, security, communication, advertising integrations or AI-assisted features; with third-party platforms that a Customer chooses to connect; and with authorities where disclosure is required by law.

We expect service providers handling information on our behalf to use it for the relevant services and to apply appropriate safeguards.

6. Data storage and transfers

Information may be stored or processed using service providers and infrastructure located in India or other countries, depending on the services used. We take steps intended to handle such information in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

7. Data retention

We keep information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide AXIOM, maintain accounts, respond to requests, meet legal requirements, resolve disputes and protect our services. When information is no longer required, we take reasonable steps to delete or anonymise it, subject to applicable legal and contractual requirements.

8. Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or alteration. Access to information is limited where appropriate to people and service providers who need it for legitimate business or operational purposes. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. We may need to verify your identity before responding. If your information is managed by a business that uses AXIOM, you may need to contact that business directly.

You can also contact us using the grievance contact details provided below for privacy-related questions or requests.

10. Children's privacy

AXIOM is intended for business use and is not designed for children. We do not knowingly seek to collect children's personal information through the Platform. If you believe such information has been provided to us improperly, please contact us.

11. Customer-managed information

Business customers may use AXIOM to manage information about their leads, prospects and customers. Those businesses are responsible for the information they submit and for providing any notices or obtaining any permissions required by law. NeoNexus handles such information to provide the AXIOM services and in accordance with the applicable customer arrangement and law.

12. Grievance Officer

For privacy-related questions, requests or grievances, you may contact the Grievance Officer appointed by NeoNexus Innovations.
