This page explains how to request deletion of personal data associated with AXIOM. Read it together with the AXIOM Privacy Policy.

1. Submit a request

Email connect@neonexusinnovations.com with the subject line AXIOM — Personal Data Deletion Request. Include your name, the email address or phone number associated with the relevant account or interaction, the business/workspace involved (if known), and a description of the information you want deleted.

Do not send passwords, one-time passcodes, payment authentication details or identity documents through ordinary email. If further verification is needed, the team will provide an appropriate method.

2. Verification

We may need to verify your identity or authority before acting. If information was provided to AXIOM by a business customer, we may need to coordinate with that business to locate the relevant lead record and determine responsibility for responding.

3. Review and response

The responsible team will review the request, identify relevant systems and service providers, and take steps required by applicable law. We may ask for more details if the request does not allow us to locate the data. The company will respond within applicable legal timelines.

4. Information that may be retained

Some information may need to be retained where required or permitted by law, for security, fraud prevention, accounting, dispute resolution or legal claims. Where information cannot be erased, we will explain why to the extent required by law.

5. Information controlled by a business customer

If your information was submitted by a business using AXIOM—for example, after you responded to that business's advertisement—the business may be the Data Fiduciary responsible for deciding how your information is used. You may need to contact that business directly. AXIOM will handle requests for which NeoNexus is responsible and reasonably assist or forward requests where appropriate.
